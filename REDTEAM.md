This gym does not establish that better research earns a reliably higher score. Its substitute data do not preserve the paper's image–measurement relationship, and the reference's hidden-test AUC ranges from **0 to 75 across three seeds**. Longer training improved the training objective but worsened hidden disease scores. Weaker controls scored lower, but by less than the seed spread. No genuinely better clinical method was established by these controls; lower training loss alone is not evidence of better generalization. Separately, several shortcuts beat the reference, including constant outputs with chance AUC.

The reproduction only partially matches the paper's reported direction. On the cached/seed-7 hidden slice, full EchoVLM improves AUC over CLIP-only **50 → 75** and the local negation metric **+0.455300 → −0.764600**, but precision **falls 50 → 42.86**, and recall **ties at 100**. The paper's Table 6 reports improvements in all three disease metrics: AUC 79.6 → 86.5, precision 29.0 → 34.2, and recall 73.6 → 86.2. The local improvement therefore does not reproduce the complete reported pattern, and its 25-point AUC gain is smaller than the 75-point seed range. Only the seed-7 baseline was rerun; no paired multi-seed baseline claim is made.

The implementation retains the recognizable computational steps in paper §3.2/Algorithm 1: symmetric image–text contrastive loss, same-view image contrastive loss, text-negation BCE, and their weighted sum. Material departures from the paper are:

- **Data and curation:** 93 paper-figure JPEG assets replace 19,065 clinically grounded image–caption pairs from 1,572 patients. Index arithmetic assigns measurements and six cycling views; no image OCR, report linkage, clinical view classification, LLM caption verification, or guideline consistency adjudication is performed. Six synthetic binary disease categories replace nine clinical severity-graded categories, including a synthetic mitral-regurgitation rule despite the paper's documented exclusions.
- **No preserved visual signal:** `measurements_for(index)` and `view = VIEW_LABELS[index % 6]` ignore pixels. Disease labels follow the assigned measurements, and captions explicitly list their positive diagnoses. Reordering source filenames would change the supposed clinical truth. Thus a better predictor here need not be a better echocardiography method; the text-only shortcut below is perfect without an image.
- **Models and initialization:** randomly initialized compact two-layer Transformers of width 128 replace the pretrained ViT-B vision backbone and CLIP text encoder initialized from internal echo and EchoCLIP weights. Text uses a local word tokenizer and 48-token truncation rather than the original CLIP text stack.
- **Training scale:** batch 32 and warmup 20 replace batch 512 and warmup 200; 65 training records yield only two batches per epoch after dropping the final record. The main paper says weight decay 0.05 while Appendix Table 15 says 0.005; the reproduction uses the appendix's 0.005. Twenty epochs, learning rate 1e−4, AdamW, cosine scheduling, and loss weights 0.5/0.1 otherwise match the stated setup.
- **Task and split:** the substitute uses 65/14/14 records rather than the paper's 80/10/10 split. The public/hidden slices each use seven of the 14 substitute test records. A single disease is chosen per image by cycling sorted disease keys using `source_index`; evaluation compares that image with its full paired caption and complementary caption. The paper evaluates positive/negative prompts for each disease. The hidden test has just three positive and four negative examples, or 12 positive–negative AUC comparisons.
- **Evidence scope:** inference is not rerun during evaluation. The evaluator scores submitted embeddings and labels, and the added negation dot-product metric is not a reported paper metric. Retrieval is diagnostic only; downstream view classification, segmentation, landmark detection, and the paper's broader clinical validation are absent.

The reference can be exceeded numerically, but the scale is not calibrated to scientific difficulty. Among the **four scored metrics, recall alone already sits at its upper bound, 100**. AUC has 25 points of headroom and precision 57.14 points. A true normalized negation cosine would have a lower bound of −1, leaving 0.235400 of headroom, but the implemented dot product has **no lower bound**. The reference's diagnostic hard-negative accuracy is also 100, with coefficient zero. CLIP-only is below the reference overall, while the much simpler caption-reading shortcut reaches AUC/precision/recall 100/100/100. This is not a meaningful clinical difficulty scale.

The constant scaled shortcut answers the aggregate-score check directly. In this measured cohort, its AUC loss contributes **−0.951059**, but its meaningless negation value contributes **+1.173936**, for total **+0.222877**. Standard-deviation normalization does not make the rank increase without limit as vector magnitudes increase; it still allows an outlier in this unconstrained metric to outweigh failure on the task. It also reduces ordinary negation differences to negligible contributions. The arithmetic uses the metric's lower direction and absolute coefficient, not a favorable sign mistake.

The valid-run discovery check passes within its filename convention: the script enumerates every `checkpoints/*/test_embeddings.jsonl`, and each successfully evaluated discovered path appears under `results` with all four scored metrics. It does not discover a method with no such file. Nor does it satisfy the failure contract: a missing artifact is silently omitted, while an empty or null artifact aborts the entire evaluator before writing any scores, including scores for otherwise valid methods. There is no explicit worst-value assignment per metric. A direct score-file forgery failed on a successful run; whether an external scheduler rejects failures or retains stale files is not claimed here. Timings and success flags are not scored inputs.

The comparison side is also not fixed: the baseline artifact replacement was accepted. However, rank improvements are anchored to the reference rather than the concurrently submitted baseline, so this manipulation only produces the explicitly labeled shortcut tie. No baseline degradation is counted as a numerical improvement.

The brute-force shortcut completed its full enumeration and hidden evaluation in about **0.43 seconds**. EchoVLM does not replace a combinatorial search, so this is not a claimed algorithmic speed comparison. Exposed answers select the winning assignment; without answers, public-slice agreement would leave the seven hidden bits undetermined. Together with perfect caption-only prediction, 12 AUC comparisons, and the huge seed spread, the run demonstrates that this fixture is too small and too exposed to support the intended research ranking.

All shortcut constructors use visible data, the visible output schema, and, for the artifact transformations, reference outputs obtainable from the improve-mode training code. None reads hidden IDs, hidden evaluator source, the paper, or ranking definitions to construct predictions. The paper and `reference.json` were read only for the requested reproduction and scoring audit. There is an additional from-scratch usability issue: `baseline/random_clip.py` delegates to `method.train`, which is absent when `method/` starts empty.

The next iteration should ground labels and views in actual image content, use a substantially larger hidden cohort with seed uncertainty, keep labels/IDs and the compared baseline evaluator-owned, reject altered coverage and malformed/nonfinite/unnormalized embeddings, and emit explicit worst outcomes for failed methods. Computing normalized cosine would remove the scaling shortcut, but would not make constant antipodes evidence of clinical negation understanding; that constraint needs task-grounded testing. No fixes to the gym itself were made in this audit.
